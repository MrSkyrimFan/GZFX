PRODUCT INTENT
======================================================================

GZFX is NOT being made runtime-compatible with ENB or ReShade.

ENB and ReShade were architecture references.

0.0.27 field configuration is standalone GZFX.

The new d3d11.dll belongs to GZFX.

Do not leave another ENB/ReShade d3d11.dll in the Skyrim root for this field
test.

======================================================================
WHY 0.0.27 EXISTS
======================================================================

0.0.26 real Skyrim telemetry showed:

    live Skyrim = 1.7.104.0
    stable shader/resource identity worked
    command-list ownership was completely coherent
    global TAA = 9,103/9,103 active frames

It also showed:

    some core resources remained unknown/preexisting;
    dinput8 lazy-load timing could be too late;
    interior HDR used a full-res HDR descendant before reduction;
    HDR detector stopped at 3x2 instead of the real 2x2 terminal;
    BeforeTonemap therefore never fired;
    clean shutdown marker remained absent.

0.0.27 fixes those architecture/detector issues.

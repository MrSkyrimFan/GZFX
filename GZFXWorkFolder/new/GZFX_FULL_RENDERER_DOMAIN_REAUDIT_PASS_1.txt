======================================================================
1. WHY THIS RE-AUDIT EXISTS
======================================================================

The owner correctly rejected the statement that GZFX was broadly ready to
begin the full major-engineering campaign required to beat ENB + ReShade.

That statement confused two different thresholds:

A. enough renderer ownership to begin some serious effect engineering;

B. enough renderer-domain knowledge to begin the complete better-than-ENB +
   ReShade product campaign without knowingly leaving water, shadows, sky,
   clouds, celestial lighting, fire, particles, weather or runtime families
   half mapped.

The user's requirement is B.

Under that requirement, the prior broad green light was premature.

This pass goes back through:

- directly reprocessed raw 0.0.28, 0.0.29 and 0.0.30 telemetry sessions;
- repository-preserved 0.0.19 manual-capture findings;
- all three repository-preserved 0.0.24 continuous-telemetry DATA passes,
  including the 417,196,650-byte archive analysis representing more than
  7 GB of uncompressed telemetry;
- the 0.0.24 late-family matrix;
- Research Pass 7 named Skyrim renderer-anchor findings;
- current public Skyrim renderer signposts used only as independent semantic
  references.

Raw 0.0.28-0.0.30 bytes were directly reprocessed in this pass.

The older 0.0.19-0.0.24 Library ZIPs were located, including the 417 MB archive,
but this execution environment did not expose an authorized raw-byte
materialization path for those historical Library objects. Their repository-
preserved findings and matrices were therefore re-audited rather than falsely
claiming a fresh byte-level parse of unavailable historical ZIP bytes.

  existing analysis never isolated fire/emissive/particle/local-light pass
  families from the broader interior renderer population.

This is a domain-classification problem, not a no-data problem.

    VOLUMETRIC_DENSITY_B

The 0.0.24 continuous session proved a perfect full-resolution exterior R16
history ping-pong across all 7,099 exterior active frames.

A distinct later exterior-only pass then writes MAIN_HDR on every exterior
frame.

2. WATER FALSIFICATION
======================================================================

HYPOTHESIS:

    the dynamic 512x512 six-face float16 cubemap plus half-resolution
    float16 chain proves WaterDomain completely.

REJECTED.

What the evidence proves:

- a real dynamic six-face exterior reflection capture exists;
- all six faces are updated across the stable three-frame cadence;
- it is paired with a depth surface;
- a stable 1280x720 float16 draw/compute chain exists immediately before the
  exterior late apply;
- the cubemap and half-resolution surfaces are consumed by full-resolution
  exterior-only passes;
- PRE_WATER_DEPTH_CURRENT and PublishPreWaterDepth are already verified;
- public Skyrim renderer names provide candidate semantic anchors for
  Main_RenderWaterEffects, ISTemporalAA_Water, ISWaterBlend and
  ISApplyReflections.

What the evidence does NOT yet prove:

- that the cubemap is exclusively a water resource rather than a broader
  environment-reflection source;
- which pass is water surface rendering;
- which pass is water TAA;
- which pass is reflection apply;
- which pass is water blend/refraction;
- where underwater rendering starts/ends;
- which constants own Fresnel, roughness, wave normals, refraction,
  reflection intensity and underwater fog.

PASS-2 WATER CLASSIFICATION:

    REFLECTION/WATER CLUSTER = HIGH CONFIDENCE
    WATERDOMAIN COMPLETE = FALSE

Required closure path:

1. correlate Main_RenderWaterEffects with the observed exterior cluster;
2. correlate ISTemporalAA_Water with the half-resolution/history family;
3. correlate ISWaterBlend and ISApplyReflections with exact D3D families;
4. field-prove entry/exit ordering relative to PublishPreWaterDepth, MAIN_HDR,
   global TAA and physical Present;
5. separately map water surface, reflection, refraction and underwater paths;
6. lock WaterDomain only after those correlations survive multiple runs.

No broad water discovery pass is needed.

A targeted semantic-correlation pass is needed.

3. SHADOW FALSIFICATION
======================================================================

HYPOTHESIS:

    the 4096/512 shadow resources and full-resolution shadow mask prove all
    shadow engineering is ready.

REJECTED.

What the evidence proves:

- stable early shadow production exists before depth/opaque world work;
- large two-slice, small two-slice and large eight-slice R16 typeless depth
  families recur identically across 0.0.28-0.0.30;
- a stable full-resolution mask pass reads MAIN_DEPTH and the large shadow
  array families;
- Semantic Authority identifies SHADOW_MASK from the depth + shadow-array
  structure;
- SHADOW_MASK exists across day exterior, inn interior and night exterior;
- public Skyrim renderer research exposes Main_RenderShadowmasks as a direct
  semantic anchor.

What the evidence does NOT yet prove:

- exact directional cascade count/layout;
- sun versus moon directional-light selection;
- which slices/resources represent local-light shadows;
- point/spot/cube shadow ownership;
- cloud-shadow generation or application;
- shadow filtering kernel/constant ownership;
- shadow map resolution/control pathways;
- the exact field-locked AfterShadowProduction exit.

The 4096x4096 array-size-8 family currently writes one observed slice in the
available exterior snapshots. That is a strong clue, not a final semantic.

The 512x512 shadow-looking families also overlap with the dynamic reflection
capture time range. Descriptor shape alone is insufficient to name every one.

PASS-2 SHADOW CLASSIFICATION:

    SHADOW PRODUCTION + SHADOW MASK = HIGH CONFIDENCE
    COMPLETE SHADOW DOMAIN = FALSE

Required closure path:

1. anchor Main_RenderShadowmasks entry/exit;
2. bracket all shadow resource production before the full-resolution mask;
3. classify directional array/slices through controlled sun/day and moon/night
   comparisons;
4. classify local-light shadow families through interior light/fire evidence;
5. isolate cloud-shadow contribution with clear/cloudy controlled evidence;
6. identify filtering/constant-buffer ownership;
7. promote AfterShadowProduction only after exact exit correlation.

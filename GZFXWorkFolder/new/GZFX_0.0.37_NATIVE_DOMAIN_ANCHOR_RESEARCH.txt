The Main_PostProcessing observer does not blindly hook an offset as a function.
It resolves the version-aware Address Library call site, requires opcode E8,
decodes the rel32 call target, verifies executable memory, then installs MinHook
on the resolved target. Any mismatch fails closed.

This completion is still read-only and does not promote PostTonemapPreUi before
real-Skyrim event/D3D correlation.

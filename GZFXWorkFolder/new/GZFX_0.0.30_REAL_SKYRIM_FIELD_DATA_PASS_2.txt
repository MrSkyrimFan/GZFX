8. ADVERSARIAL UIPRESENT TIMING CHECK
======================================================================

Pass 1 observed at least one pipeline row whose TELEMETRY QPC is after
UiPresent on every active frame.

Pass 2 checked whether that means Skyrim actually executes the draw after the
UiPresent semantic.

It does NOT prove that.

Critical evidence:

A. Current source defines UiPresent in:

       semantic_authority::OnPresent

   and signals it with reason:

       "frame_present"

   during the Present hook callback.

B. UiPresent is therefore a PRESENT-TIME landmark, not an engine pre-UI hook.

C. The final retained pipeline row's ordinal equals frames.csv draw_ordinal on
   sampled and generalized active frames.

   That ordinal is the final Skyrim immediate-context draw ordinal already
   accumulated before Present callbacks.

D. Pipeline cartography can flush a pending pass record later than the pass's
   original execution.

Therefore:

    pipeline-row QPC > UiPresent QPC

cannot be interpreted as:

    Skyrim executed that draw after UiPresent.

This is the important Pass-2 correction.

DATA conclusion:

    UiPresent must NOT be used as proof of the real UI-start boundary.

The 0.0.30 extension is still useful because it prevents the final pending
late record from being discarded and retains callback/present evidence, but it
does not magically convert UiPresent into a true engine pre-UI marker.

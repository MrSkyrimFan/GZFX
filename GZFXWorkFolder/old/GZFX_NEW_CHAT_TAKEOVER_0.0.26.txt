======================================================================
3. WHY 0.0.26 EXISTS
======================================================================

0.0.25 real-Skyrim telemetry proved that the late renderer structure had not
disappeared.

The known HDR/TAA spine remained visible on all active scene frames.

The failures were architecture/identity problems:

    - HDR detector latched onto the wrong early MAIN_HDR downsample and could
      not recover;

    - TAA detection depended on the failed HDR/tonemap path;

    - the old UiPresent semantic marker was not the real DXGI Present;

    - deferred detailed evidence was gated off at UiPresent;

    - command lists that merely lacked a GZFX tag were mislabeled "Skyrim";

    - the dominant supposed "Skyrim" list was almost certainly GZFX visible
      pass work;

    - shaderHashes sometimes contained stable content hashes and sometimes
      runtime COM pointers.

Research of ENB's shipped SDK/FX, 3Dmigoto, Nukem9, Community Shaders,
ReShade, PureDark Skyrim Upscaler and Microsoft D3D11 established the next
architecture:

    stable creation-time identity
    + Skyrim engine epochs
    + D3D resource/pass graph
    + explicit ownership
    + physical Present timeline.

======================================================================
4. WHAT 0.0.26 IMPLEMENTS
======================================================================

A. Creation-time identity

GZFX observes D3D11 creation of:

    buffers
    1D/2D/3D textures
    VS/GS/GS-SO/PS/HS/DS/CS shaders
    deferred contexts.

It does not mutate descriptors or bytecode.

It records creator module, birth and exact shader bytecode identity.

B. Shader identity correction

Stable content hash and process-local object identity are now separate.

Never treat a shader COM pointer as a stable hash.

C. Command-list ownership

Known GZFX producers are explicitly tagged:

    Wave3
    Wave4
    visible pass
    resource diagnostics
    semantic diagnostics.

Untagged work is called:

    external

not:

    Skyrim.

D. Deferred telemetry

DeferredList / DeferredRun evidence is retained even when the immediate
late-detail gate is off.

E. Physical Present timeline

New:

    present_timeline.csv

with:

    hook_enter
    callbacks_begin
    callbacks_end
    before_original_present
    after_original_present.

Only primary/process-window swap chain creates physical present epochs.

F. Skyrim engine epochs

New fail-closed module supports only verified:

    Steam AE 1.6.1170.0

Initial observational anchors:

    post_processing
    pre_ui

No SKSE.
No Address Library.

Unsupported runtime:

    no engine hook;
    D3D telemetry continues.

G. HDR/TAA repair

HDR can abandon a poisoned reduction candidate and restart from a later valid
MAIN_HDR descendant.

Global TAA structural recognition no longer depends on HDR or tonemap
completion.

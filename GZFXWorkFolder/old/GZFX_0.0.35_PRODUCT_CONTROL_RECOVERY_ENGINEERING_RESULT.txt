======================================================================

The owner reported that current effect sliders moved and saved, but produced no
visible rendering changes.

non-default values:

    AmbientContact Strength=2.000
    AmbientContact RadiusPixels=24.00

    HorizonOcclusion Enabled=1
    HorizonOcclusion Strength=1.000
    HorizonOcclusion RadiusPixels=28.00
    HorizonOcclusion Quality=2

    IndirectBounce Enabled=1
    IndirectBounce Strength=0.600
    IndirectBounce RadiusPixels=36.00
    IndirectBounce Saturation=1.500

    LocalGlow Enabled=1
    LocalGlow Strength=1.375
    LocalGlow Threshold=0.250
    LocalGlow RadiusPixels=8.00

Therefore the failure was downstream of mouse/menu persistence.

pre_ui_effects.txt proved the exact runtime condition:

    status=skipped_wave4_owns_mode
    injections=0
    mode=2

and simultaneously recorded the changed AmbientContact/LocalGlow values.

Current source then proved the architectural disconnect:

A. SceneEffects returned immediately whenever AV2 owned mode 1/2.
   Therefore it never reached:
       AmbientContact
       LocalGlow
       legacy ambient-depth / indirect stack

B. AmbientVisibilityV2 owned modes 1/2 but did not include settings.hpp.
   Its artistic parameters were hard-coded.

C. visible_pass.cpp retained an AmbientContact fallback, but disabled that
   fallback whenever AV2 successfully applied.

Result:

    settings changed correctly;
    no active renderer owner consumed them coherently.

This was a control-plane/render-owner disconnect, not a mouse/UI defect.

======================================================================
0. RESEARCH PURPOSE
======================================================================

Research Pass 2 was intentionally narrow.

It was not another general survey of Skyrim's renderer.

It was designed specifically to answer the blockers exposed by the 0.0.19
real-Skyrim field gate:

1. What are the real roles of the two MAIN_DEPTH copy resources?
2. Why does the current resource called OPAQUE_FINAL_DEPTH appear only
   outdoors and only after AO / indirect composition?
3. What exactly should "AfterCameraZ" mean?
4. How should a semantic registry distinguish a known resource identity from
   a resource that is actually valid for the current frame/stage?
5. Why do CameraZ, RawAO and indirect lighting continue indoors without the
   later exterior depth copy?
6. Is the currently named "lighting composite" actually a generic lighting
   composite, or a more specific Skyrim image-space stage?

The research combines:

A. GZFX's own field chronology from GZFX23222111.zip.

B. Public Skyrim renderer type information from CommonLibSSE-NG.

C. Public reverse-engineering and decompile-transcription work in current
   Community Shaders.

D. Secondary public Skyrim renderer projects only where they corroborate
   target inventories or naming.

Field evidence remains authoritative for what actually happened in Lance's
Skyrim process.

Public research is used to assign or refine meaning where it structurally
matches the field graph.

5. MAJOR RESOLUTION:
THE LATER "OPAQUE_FINAL_DEPTH" RESOURCE IS A VOLUMETRIC PREVIOUS-DEPTH HISTORY
BEFORE ITS COPY
======================================================================

This is the largest correction from Research Pass 2.

The 0.0.19 field findings showed a later exterior-only resource currently
named:

OPAQUE_FINAL_DEPTH

Example identity:

0x1FEE0DC4D90

The field chronology looked strange:

CameraZ
-> RawAO
-> AO filter
-> indirect / composite
-> later MAIN_DEPTH copy into 0x1FEE0DC4D90

That made the static "final opaque depth" interpretation suspicious.

The exact pass immediately BEFORE the copy resolves the mystery.

Example:
manual capture 0001
frame 2483
pass immediately before MAIN_DEPTH -> 0x1FEE0DC4D90

The pass reads:

t0:
MAIN_DEPTH

t1:
a full-resolution R16_FLOAT previous/intermediate volumetric surface

t2:
320x192x90 R16_FLOAT 3D volumetric-lighting density volume

t3:
4096x1 R32_FLOAT repartition lookup

t4:
RGBA8 noise / gradient texture

t5:
MOTION vectors

t6:
0x1FEE0DC4D90
the resource GZFX had called OPAQUE_FINAL_DEPTH

The pass writes:

a full-resolution R16_FLOAT volumetric-lighting result.


Public reconstructed shader:

Community Shaders
package/Shaders/ISApplyVolumetricLighting.hlsl

declares exactly:

t0 DepthTex
t1 PreviousFrameTex
t2 VLTex
t3 RepartitionTex
t4 NoiseGradSamplerTex
t5 MotionVectorsTex

The HLSL:

1. reads current depth;
2. samples the 3D volumetric-light field;
3. reprojects into previous-frame UV using motion;
4. samples previous volumetric result;
5. samples PreviousDepthTex;
6. rejects temporal history based on current-vs-previous depth disagreement.

The GZFX field pass is therefore a direct slot-for-slot structural match to:

ISApplyVolumetricLighting

and GZFX's mystery resource at t6 is functionally:

PreviousDepthTex

at that moment in the frame.

This is much stronger than an enum-name guess.

RESEARCH CONCLUSION:

Before the later CopyResource:

the physical resource previously called OPAQUE_FINAL_DEPTH contains
PREVIOUS-FRAME DEPTH HISTORY FOR THE EXTERIOR VOLUMETRIC TEMPORAL PASS.

Functional semantic:

VOLUMETRIC_PREVIOUS_DEPTH

Confidence:
VERY HIGH / functionally identified from field + public shader structure.

6. SAME PHYSICAL RESOURCE, NEW CONTENT EPOCH AFTER THE COPY
======================================================================

Immediately after ISApplyVolumetricLighting consumes the resource as
PreviousDepthTex, Skyrim performs:

MAIN_DEPTH
->
that same physical resource

This means the physical texture changes semantic content within one frame.

BEFORE COPY:

resource contains previous-frame scene depth
and is consumed as:
VolumetricPreviousDepth

AFTER COPY:

resource contains current-frame MAIN_DEPTH snapshot
and becomes the depth preserved for later stages and/or next frame's
volumetric previous-depth use.

This is the key architectural correction:

RESOURCE IDENTITY != SEMANTIC CONTENT EPOCH

A single Semantic enum permanently attached to one COM texture cannot fully
describe this resource.

The registry needs stage/content-epoch awareness.

8. WHY THE LATER DEPTH RESOURCE DISAPPEARS IN INTERIORS
======================================================================

The field mystery is now straightforward.

Interiors do not execute Skyrim's large exterior volumetric-lighting system.

Therefore they do not execute the same:

ISApplyVolumetricLighting temporal path

that requires:

PreviousFrameTex
MotionVectors
PreviousDepthTex
3D volumetric density

The associated previous-depth/current-depth copy cycle is absent.

Meanwhile interiors still execute:

CameraZ
RawAO
AO filtering
RawIndirect
ambient/screen-space composition

because those systems are driven by the early scene-depth / CameraZ path.

Therefore the absence of the later depth copy indoors is not evidence that
interiors lack usable scene depth.

It means:

that later resource belongs to an EXTERIOR VOLUMETRIC / PRE-WATER DEPTH DOMAIN,
not the universal stock SAO depth domain.

This cleanly resolves Field Finding #2.

11. CAMERA-Z PROMOTION AND CAMERA-Z COMPLETION ARE DIFFERENT EVENTS
======================================================================

0.0.19 currently ties AfterCameraZ signaling to a successful semantic
promotion.

That is architecturally wrong.

Promotion means:

"We learned that this physical resource is CameraZ."

That normally occurs once or only when identity changes.

Stage completion means:

"The CameraZ hierarchy for THIS FRAME is now complete and safe for this
frame's downstream consumers."

That must happen every frame.

This explains the field result:

CAMERAZ resource:
fresh in 19 / 19 captures

AfterCameraZ scheduler events:
0

The resource was already known, so no new promotion happened.

RESEARCH CONCLUSION:

AfterCameraZ must be an execution-stage event, not a discovery event.

======================================================================
12. CORRECT AfterCameraZ CONTRACT
======================================================================

The corrected scheduler contract should be:

AfterCameraZ fires exactly once per applicable frame when the active CameraZ
hierarchy for that frame has completed.

Requirements:

1. Resource identity can already be known.

2. Event must be independent of promotion.

3. Track the current frame's CameraZ write coverage.

4. Determine required mip coverage from the resource descriptor rather than
   assuming exactly 12 forever.

5. On the currently observed flat Skyrim path:
   - observe mip 0;
   - observe all required subsequent mips;
   - fire AfterCameraZ after the final required mip write.

6. If a compute CameraZ+mips path is encountered:
   - recognize that execution structure separately;
   - fire the same logical AfterCameraZ contract when its output hierarchy is
     complete.

7. Reset per-frame completion state at frame change.

8. Never fire merely because a CameraZ resource identity exists.

The scheduler should represent renderer execution, not knowledge acquisition.

15. STOCK CameraZ IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY THE IDEAL FUTURE GZFX AO/GI DEPTH
======================================================================

Research Pass 1 treated stock CameraZ as a highly valuable native hierarchy.

That remains true.

However Research Pass 2 introduces an important quality caveat.

Community Shaders commit #2787 explicitly documents a problem encountered by
their own post-geometry systems:

the earlier z-prepass depth can omit alpha-tested geometry such as:

- rocks
- cliffs
- road edges

Their fix was to publish a later finished opaque depth for:

- SSGI
- SSS
- their composite
- water

This is public evidence about why a prepass-based depth representation can be
insufficient for high-quality screen-space effects.

GZFX field evidence shows stock CameraZ is created from the early copy.

Therefore:

Stock CameraZ is a VALID stock-renderer semantic and an excellent landmark.

But it should NOT automatically become the permanent production depth
hierarchy for GZFX Ambient Visibility v2 or GI.

A higher-quality GZFX architecture should investigate using:

current MAIN_DEPTH after opaque MRT geometry is complete

to create a GZFX-owned hierarchical depth representation that includes the
geometry actually present at that point.

This is a blueprint decision, not an implementation instruction yet.

17. SEMANTIC FRESHNESS:
PUBLIC RESEARCH SUPPORTS A FRAME-VALIDITY CONTRACT
======================================================================

The 0.0.19 field findings showed that the registry preserves old identities
for resources that are not active in the current environment.

Examples during interiors:

VOLUMETRIC_DENSITY_A
VOLUMETRIC_DENSITY_B

remain known in the session registry even though they were last produced in a
prior exterior frame.

This is useful for structural memory.

It is unsafe for a production broker if identity != 0 is treated as "usable."

Public renderer projects repeatedly use frame-number validity rules.

Examples from Community Shaders:

State:
RequestHistoryReset / ShouldResetHistory use explicit frame ownership.

ExponentialHeightFog:
temporal history is accepted only when:

current frame == last producing frame + 1

Grass culling:
a cached result is considered current only when:

lastFrame == current frame

These reinforce the correct GZFX design:

KNOWING WHAT A RESOURCE IS

must be separate from:

THAT RESOURCE BEING VALID NOW

- confidence

- lastProducedFrame

- validFromStage
- validUntilStage
- contentEpoch
- currentFrameAvailable


The Native Semantic Broker must query BOTH.

A known resource that was not produced/observed for the current requested

20. CONTENT EPOCHS ARE REQUIRED FOR MULTI-ROLE RESOURCES
======================================================================

The kPOST_ZPREPASS_COPY-like resource proves that freshness alone is not
enough.

The same physical resource has at least two roles in one exterior frame:

Epoch A:
before current MAIN_DEPTH copy
=
previous-frame depth history for volumetric temporal reprojection

Epoch B:
after current MAIN_DEPTH copy
=
current-frame pre-water / post-z-prepass depth

Therefore the semantic authority needs the concept of:

CONTENT EPOCH

A resource can remain the same COM object and generation while its semantic
meaning changes after a known producer event.

Suggested conceptual model:

ResourceIdentity:
one physical texture

SemanticView A:
VolumetricPreviousDepth
valid before `PublishPreWaterDepth`

SemanticView B:
PostZPrepassDepthCurrent
valid after `PublishPreWaterDepth`

This is a better model than assigning one forever-name to the texture.

32. RESEARCH PASS 2 FINAL CONCLUSION
======================================================================

Research Pass 2 resolves the largest 0.0.19 contradiction.

The later exterior-only resource was not a universal depth buffer that
mysteriously appeared too late.

It is a stage-dependent temporal resource.

Before the per-frame copy it is consumed by Skyrim's exterior volumetric
temporal pass as PreviousDepthTex.

Immediately afterward MAIN_DEPTH is copied into the same physical texture,
making it the current pre-water/post-z-prepass depth snapshot and the next
frame's history source.

This explains:

- its late position;
- its exterior-only field behavior;
- its absence indoors;
- why CameraZ/AO/indirect run perfectly without it indoors.

Research also establishes that:

- CameraZ resource promotion and CameraZ frame completion must be separated;
- the current generic LightingComposite scheduler event is actually a strong
  SAO/AmbientScreenSpaceComposite match;
- semantic identities need current-frame validity and content epochs;
- the next architecture should investigate a GZFX-owned hierarchy built from
  MAIN_DEPTH after opaque MRT completion rather than blindly depending on
  stock prepass CameraZ for final production AO/GI.

The 0.0.19 foundation was not a dead end.

Its field gate exposed exactly where the semantic model was still too static.
